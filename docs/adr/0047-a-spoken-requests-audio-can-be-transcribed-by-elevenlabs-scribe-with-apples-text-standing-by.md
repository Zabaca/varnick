# A spoken request's audio can be transcribed by ElevenLabs' Scribe v2, per Mac and off by default, with Apple's text standing by

Builds on [ADR 0033](0033-spoken-answers-can-be-voiced-by-elevenlabs-and-the-installed-voice-is-the-fallback.md) (the ElevenLabs key, its kept connection and failure sorting), [ADR 0043](0043-voice-mode-on-the-phone-page-talks-to-a-macs-voice-session-through-its-relay-and-is-kept-alive-by-a-quiet-tone.md) (the phone's segments) and [ADR 0044](0044-on-a-mac-hey-varnick-or-a-key-starts-voice-mode-in-which-every-mic-line-is-a-request-until-it-ends.md) (a request that ends only at "over"). Option 01 of `spoken-requests-get-words-wrong` is the spec.

The user reported that Apple's on-device transcriber hears him but gets words and names wrong, most of all in the car. A new switch in Settings › Listening, **Transcribe requests with ElevenLabs**, off by default and kept per Mac, has ElevenLabs' Scribe v2 (batch) turn the audio of each spoken request into text beside Apple's transcriber. Scribe's text is used when it comes back within 2 s of both being started; otherwise, or on a failure, a refusal or no network, Apple's is. The user's decisions of 2026-11-02: one switch per Mac; the phone's Voice mode follows its Mac's switch and Hey Varnick follows its own Mac's; requests only, never Listening's whole day; the wake phrase stays Apple's; he accepts that a request's audio goes to ElevenLabs and is kept there under its retention policy, and the per-minute spend.

- **What is sent.** A segment the phone's Voice mode sends (16 kHz mono 16-bit, as it arrives), and, at the Mac, each final microphone result that becomes part of a request for the voice session: the one holding the wake phrase and each later one, or in Voice mode each line taken as a request. The audio of a line is cut from the last minute of the microphone, which Listening holds in memory (`MicWindow`) only while the switch, a saved key, Listening and Hey Varnick are on and the voice session answers, and never writes. Nothing before the wake phrase, nothing of the Mac's own audio, nothing of Listening's day transcript. Audio quieter than the silence floor, or under 100 ms, goes to neither transcriber.
- **What Scribe is told.** The model (`scribe_v2`, pinned, no picker), English, raw 16 kHz PCM, no audio-event tags, speaker labels or timestamps, and keyterms: "Varnick" and the names the user lists in Settings, at most 100.
- **The choice.** `Transcription.choose` (Packages/VarnickPeer): Scribe's text when it came within `Transcription.deadline` (2 s, to be set by the hand test), even blank; Apple's otherwise; Scribe's when Apple failed and Scribe answered; a failure when both failed. At the Mac the request is collected on Apple's text as before (the wake phrase, "over", the cap, the minute, the echo check), and when it is dispatched its text is made again from its lines (`Transcription.request`), each line Scribe's or Apple's, a line of Scribe's that lacks the wake phrase it should start with or the "over" it should end with giving way to Apple's. Dispatch waits only for lines still out, each up to its deadline. Varnick's own steps (Settings › Experimental) keep Apple's text.
- **Failures.** Each piece tries ElevenLabs afresh; nothing rests it. The last failure shows under the switch, in memory, worded for minutes and for the Speech to Text permission, which the key may lack.

## Considered Options

- **Scribe v2 Realtime (option 02), and Apple's dictation model taught the user's names (option 03):** not built; they stay proposed.
- **Sending each Mac request's whole audio once, after "over":** rejected. One call, but the wait is one call over the whole request and the silences between lines are billed. The lines go as Apple finishes them.
- **Calling Apple only when Scribe fails:** rejected. Apple's pass would then cost its own wait after Scribe's failure; running both at once makes the fallback free.
- **Transcribing Listening's whole day with Scribe:** rejected. Every word the microphone hears all day would leave the Mac, against "what you say and hear stays on your Mac" for everyone who only wants better requests.
- **A switch on the phone page:** not now. The Mac holds the key and does the transcribing; a phone switch would need a new request to change a Mac setting.

## Consequences

- **A request's audio leaves the Mac, with the switch on, and ElevenLabs keeps it under its privacy policy;** zero retention is for enterprise plans only. Every segment the phone sends while Voice mode runs goes (a passenger, the radio, and in Car mode the car's speakers included), and at the Mac each line from the wake phrase to "over", and in Voice mode every line that becomes a request. `docs/privacy-and-security.md` says so, and so does the switch's text.
- **It amends three decisions.** [ADR 0015](0015-spoken-requests-are-answered-locally-aloud-only-through-listening.md): a spoken request's audio can now leave the Mac. [ADR 0033](0033-spoken-answers-can-be-voiced-by-elevenlabs-and-the-installed-voice-is-the-fallback.md): "never audio from the Mac" holds only with the switch off. [ADR 0043](0043-voice-mode-on-the-phone-page-talks-to-a-macs-voice-session-through-its-relay-and-is-kept-alive-by-a-quiet-tone.md): speech becomes text with Listening's transcriber or, with the switch on, with ElevenLabs, and "the transcriber is on-device" holds for Apple's alone. Each carries a dated amendment line.
- **The key needs the Speech to Text permission as well** as Text to Speech and Voices Read. Without it Apple's text is used and the note under the switch names the permission.
- **It costs per second of audio sent, silence included:** $0.22 an hour plus $0.05 for keyterms, drawn first from the plan's monthly allowance that spoken answers also use. When the allowance runs out Apple's text is used. Estimated under $3 a month at 30 requests a day; unmeasured.
- **A little slower,** estimated half a second to a second on the segment that ends with "over", never more than 2 s; unmeasured.
- **Two texts of one request.** The day's transcript keeps Apple's `[mic]` lines; the voice session's prompt and the phone's view have the chosen text.
- **Waking and "over" at the Mac stay Apple's.** A wake phrase Apple misses is not helped.
- **The mic callback now also hands each converted buffer to a second serial queue** (the window), under the rule Save audio follows.
