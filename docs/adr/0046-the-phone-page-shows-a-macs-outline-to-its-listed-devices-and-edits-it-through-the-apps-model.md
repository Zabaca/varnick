# The Phone page shows a Mac's Outline to its listed devices and edits it through the app's model, and holds its text in the page's memory alone

Amends [ADR 0030](0030-the-outline-is-one-json-file-varnick-owns-of-bullets-and-placements-never-written-over-when-unreadable.md) (the Outline is "local only": nothing reaches the phone), [ADR 0031](0031-sessions-read-and-act-on-varnick-through-its-own-model-on-the-varnick-server-with-no-grant.md) (`outline_read`: "Nothing reaches a paired Mac, the phone or TypeSafe", and the Outline "edited by its own keys alone") and [ADR 0011](0011-each-host-serves-the-phone-page-behind-tailscale-serve.md) (the page's requests and what it keeps). Option 03 of `outline-out-of-reach-from-the-phone` is the spec, and the user's answers of 2026-10-20 (relayed by the coordinator) are recorded in it: full editing; "Yes, allow it" to the Outline's text reaching the phone over the tailnet, held in the page's memory only and never cached, logged or pushed; "Yes, all defaults".

The Outline (`CONTEXT.md`) was a Mac's own: a file, a panel, and since ADR 0031 the sessions of that Mac. It is now also on that Mac's **Phone page**, read and edited by the user's listed devices:

- **Two relay requests (protocol 34), each one short process.** `outlineRead` lists the rows of the phone's view; `outlineEdit` applies a text or note save and one act, and lists the rows again. A Mac's relay forwards them to its running Varnick over the socket, as it does Voice mode's, and the app answers on the main actor from its own model (`OutlineRemote`). Only a **listed device** may ask. A paired Mac is refused by the relay and by the app: its Varnick has no view of another Mac's Outline, so allowing it would only add a reader. A headless host has none (its code is the Mac app's) and refuses, saying it needs a Mac.
- **Each Mac's page shows that Mac's own Outline.** The page asks its own host's relay and no one else (ADR 0011). With the Outline switch off, or the file unreadable or from a newer Varnick, the request is refused in the Outline's own words, and nothing is loaded or written.
- **The view is the phone's.** It sends its zoom (placement ids), the placements it opened and closed, Show completed, and where a continuing read starts, with every request. The app applies them to a copy of the tree, lists the rows that copy shows with the tree's own rules (the page never works out which rows show), and drops the copy. The Mac's own zoom, collapse and Show completed, its rows and its caret are untouched, and the phone's collapse is never saved to the file or put on the undo stack. The first read starts at Home with the Mac's saved collapse and the Mac's Show completed. A zoom that has left the tree opens at Home, and the reply says so.
- **Bounded.** At most 400 rows and 700,000 bytes of rows a reply, then a path to continue after; a text of at most 10,000 characters and a note of at most 100,000 bytes are listed whole, a longer one cut and flagged, and the page does not edit a cut one. A request is one base64 argument of at most 120,000 bytes, so a text, a note or pasted lines are held to 60,000 bytes of JSON string and the view's opened and closed placements to 300.
- **Edits go through the edit sessions use.** Every act and save is `Outline.performForSession`, the edit Panel access's `outline_act` uses (ADR 0031): one step on the Mac's undo stack, so ⌘Z on the Mac takes back a phone edit; the Mac user's caret moved only where the same key would move it; a change to the bullet the Mac's caret is in goes into that text view around the caret, and waits while an input method is composing; the panel never shown, raised or focused; the Outline switch asked right before the edit. The acts are add (the Mac's paste rules, after a bullet, inside one, in place of an empty one, or at the end of the phone's zoom), complete (set to a state, not toggled), Enter at a UTF-16 offset (the tree's split, or the first nested bullet for the phone's title), delete, indent, outdent and move. A text save and the act after it (Enter, or a key of the row) travel in one request and are applied in order, as two undo steps. Enter follows the phone's collapse of the row, not the Mac's, as one edit: open, the text after the caret becomes the first nested bullet; closed, the next sibling.
- **A save never overwrites what changed on the Mac.** Each row of a reply carries a stamp of its text and of its note, a SHA-256 digest of the field's text, the same across launches. A save sends the stamp it started from, the app compares it right before the edit, and a mismatch refuses the save and replies with the field as it is. The phone keeps what it typed in its field and shows the Mac's version in a card, and Use mine saves it again with the new stamp. The app makes every stamp; the page never computes one, and takes a field's new stamp only from the reply to its own save. A page keeps no state per phone for this: the stamp travels with the request. An Enter or a delete carries the stamp of the text the phone read too (a ⌫ in an empty bullet says it must still be empty), and a delete says whether the phone showed bullets nested, so a row that changed on the Mac is not split or deleted from a stale view.
- **Structure goes by the whole tree with the phone's Show completed.** Indent, outdent and move treat siblings as siblings whatever is collapsed, as a session's do, and step over completed siblings as the phone shows them, not as the Mac does (`OutlineScope.showsCompleted`). The tree knows only the Mac's zoom, so the phone's title can be completed and edited and nothing else, and no row outdents past the top of the phone's zoom; the page and the app both hold these. A bullet the Mac is zoomed into is its title for the phone as for a session: it can't be moved or deleted until the Mac zooms out, and Enter in it adds a first nested bullet.
- **The text's way to the phone,** and where it stops: the app, the relay on the same Mac, the page's server on an owner-only socket (or loopback, where the Mac's `tailscale serve` can't serve a socket), `tailscale serve` over HTTPS on the tailnet, the phone's browser. The phone holds it in the page's memory while the view shows, and drops it when the view closes. **The page's worker caches nothing, the replies are `no-store`, and no bullet, note, stamp or typed text goes into the page's storage, a push, a notification, the address (which holds placement ids) or a log.** The server's access line is the method, the path without a query, the status, the device and the time; the relay logs nothing of a request; the app's line holds the request's kind, its outcome and counts. The only thing the page keeps is whether Show completed is on.
- **Who can read it:** the user's listed devices, and on a Mac whose page listens on loopback, any program on that Mac, as is already true of that Mac's sessions. Anyone who sees the phone sees the Outline on it, as with every view of the page.

## Amends

- **ADR 0030, "Local only".** The file stays on the Mac, and the relay and the launch snapshot still never read it. Its bullets and notes now also reach the Phone page of that Mac, and no other machine, as above. Its amendment of 2026-10-11 ("local only holds for the file and for every Mac and device, not for a session that reads the outline") now has a second exception, the listed device. The file's format, version and the rule that it is never written over when unreadable are unchanged.
- **ADR 0031, `outline_read`'s "nothing reaches a paired Mac, the phone or TypeSafe".** A session's read still sends nothing to a paired Mac or TypeSafe; what the phone gets is its own request, not a session's. And **"edited by its own keys alone"** was already amended for sessions; a listed device now edits it too, without the Mac asking, by the same edit.
- **ADR 0011.** The page's requests now include the Outline's two, the page holds the user's bullets in memory while its view shows, and the one preference it keeps for the view (Show completed) is a flag.

## Considered Options

- **Read only (option 01), and add and check off only (option 02):** rejected by the user, who wanted full editing. Their view, request and rules are part of this option.
- **One Mac's Outline on every host's page:** not chosen. It is the hub ADR 0011 turned down: one host's page would reach another Mac.
- **A paired Mac may read another Mac's Outline:** not chosen; listed devices only.
- **A phone collapse saved to the Mac, mirrors placed from the phone, undo on the phone, a join of a bullet onto the row above:** left out. Collapse is the phone's view; the other three need the Mac's selection, clipboard or rows. Delete asks first when bullets are nested, since the phone has no undo.
- **A stamp made with Swift's `Hasher` (the reason is in `docs/agents/where-it-breaks.md`), or a version counter on the file:** rejected; a counter would have to be saved with every edit.
- **Sending only the changed characters of a text:** not built. A save sends the whole field, and the app's edit replaces it with the smallest change when the Mac's caret is in it.

## Consequences

- **A protocol bump to 34.** Every Mac, the headless host and each page must run one commit; a Mac on 33 refuses.
- **The Outline's text leaves the Mac for the first time,** to the user's phone over the tailnet. The privacy document, the README, `CONTEXT.md` and the two lines in the app that said "kept on this Mac" say so.
- **The phone can change and delete the Outline.** A phone edit lands in the user's Outline without the Mac asking, as a session's does, and is saved with it.
- **Out of date until it reads again.** Nothing is pushed to the phone: the rows are read when the view opens, when the page comes back into view, on Refresh, at a zoom and when a bullet is opened or closed, and an edit lists them again. A bullet that changed on the Mac is told apart only when the phone saves over it.
- **A phone keyboard's Enter and ⌫ are read from input events,** which phone keyboards send differently (Android's in particular); ⌫ at the start of an empty bullet may not be reported at all, and the Delete key always works. This is unverified until the user tries it on the phone.
- **Saved at pauses, not letter by letter.** Each save is one relay process and one undo step on the Mac: a bullet typed with pauses is several steps, and a page killed between saves loses at most the last two seconds.
- **A long outline's new row** (an add at the end) may be past the rows listed; Show more reaches it.
