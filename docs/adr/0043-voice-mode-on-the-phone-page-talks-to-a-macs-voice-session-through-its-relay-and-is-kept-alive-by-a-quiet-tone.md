# Voice mode on the Phone page talks to a Mac's voice session through its relay, hears and speaks on the Mac, and is kept alive by a quiet tone

Builds on [ADR 0042](0042-a-spoken-request-is-answered-by-the-hosts-voice-session-one-hidden-claude-session-in-home-with-permissions-skipped.md) (the voice session this is the second surface of), [ADR 0011](0011-each-host-serves-the-phone-page-behind-tailscale-serve.md) (the page and its relay requests) and [ADR 0010](0010-a-panes-turn-comes-from-hooks-that-write-to-the-pane-folder.md) (the turn the answer is read from). Option 05 of `phone-page-is-only-a-browser-tab` is the spec.

A **Voice mode** button on a Mac host's Phone page starts a back-and-forth by voice with that Mac's **Voice session** (`CONTEXT.md`): the same session Hey Varnick reaches at the desk, so a question asked aloud there can be followed up from the phone within the 10 minutes. The user's decisions of 2026-10-16:

- **Speech becomes text, and the reply becomes voice, on the Mac host,** with Listening's transcriber and the Voice setting's voice (ElevenLabs or the installed voice). The phone sends audio and plays audio.
- **No Voice mode on a headless host at first.** It has no transcriber or voice; the page hides the button and the relay says so.
- **Foreground only,** by the browser's rules, with one measured addition (below).

- **Three relay requests (protocol 30), each one short process.** `voiceAudio` carries one chunk of an utterance (16 kHz 16-bit mono, 1 s, base64; a request is one base64 argument of at most 120,000 bytes, which is why a chunk is small), `voiceAsk` the same ask as text, and `voiceStatus` the ask's events from a count on, polled once a second as `task_run_status` is. A listed device or Mac may ask; only a Mac's app answers (`VoiceRemote`).
- **The page decides where a request ends** (silence after speech, named constants, a noise floor measured at the start so the tone and a fan are not speech) and sends it while it is still being said, so only the last chunk waits after the last word. The Mac assembles the chunks in order (`VoiceUploads`), transcribes (`VoiceTranscriber`: a single pass of `SpeechTranscriber` over the finished utterance), shows the phone what it heard, and asks the voice session as Hey Varnick does, with the phone's moment, zone and units in the hint (`VoiceSessionHost.ask`).
- **The reply comes back as the answer's text, its spoken form and its audio** as one 16 kHz mono WAV made on the Mac (`VoiceRender`, which records the Speaker's voice instead of playing it; an answer too long for a reply line goes as text alone). The page plays it through its audio context. While a request is out and its reply plays, the microphone's audio is dropped, so Varnick does not hear itself.
- **A question or plan is answered in the session's own terminal.** The event names the voice session's zmx session; the page's Open button opens it with take, as any session opens there, and the session is then the user's (ADR 0042). (Amended 2026-10-31 below: the hidden tab is no longer reached by that event alone; a Mac's Phone page lists it, marked.)
- **A quiet tone keeps the page alive.** The spike on a Pixel (Chrome 155, a tab, 2026-10-16) found a hidden, silent page frozen after 60 s (the websocket closed about 5 s later, and the page was fine on return), and a hidden page that played a 220 Hz tone at 0.02 not frozen for 132 s, its microphone hearing the user throughout and its websocket open. So Voice mode plays that tone from Start to End, and keeps the screen on while the page is shown. A page that is frozen anyway reconnects on its next resume (the microphone, the context, the tone, the screen) and says so in the view.
- **The page keeps none of the exchange** (amended 2026-10-31 below: it reads the Voice session's earlier exchanges from the Mac when the view opens). It lives in the view; the page's worker and cache hold none of it; the server logs the method, path, status, device and time; the Mac's timing line holds durations and kinds.

## Amends, for this mode

- [ADR 0011](0011-each-host-serves-the-phone-page-behind-tailscale-serve.md): the page now holds the microphone (in the foreground, from a tap) and sends the host audio, as chunks inside the relay requests it already makes; and a page may play a tone to stay unfrozen.

## Considered Options

- **Speech to text on the phone (Chrome's SpeechRecognition) and the phone's own voice.** Not chosen by the user. It would have worked on a headless host too, but SpeechRecognition ends the moment the page is hidden, and Chrome sends its audio to Google.
- **A streaming channel (a websocket relay process) for the audio.** Not built: chunks through the existing short relay requests are enough for a request of a few seconds, and nothing new has to stay open on the Mac.
- **Ending the mode when the page is hidden or the phone locks.** The option's first draft. Replaced by the tone for the hidden page; a locked screen is unmeasured (see Consequences).
- **A native Android shell with a microphone foreground service,** for a locked phone. Deferred.

## Consequences

- **Every Mac, the headless host and each Phone page must run the commit that made protocol 30.** A headless host answers the three requests with a refusal.
- **A locked phone is not known to work.** The spike's tone run was a hidden page; locked is the user's to try (the hand test), and Doze can cut the network with the screen off.
- **The tone is audible to the microphone** and sounds quiet but not silent; the noise floor keeps it from beginning a request. A phone that plays other audio may be paused by Android's audio focus.
- **Anything the phone's microphone hears can run commands on the Mac, unasked,** as at the Mac (ADR 0042). The page sends audio only when the user has tapped Start on it, and Voice mode needs the voice session on (it is, unless Answer with Varnick's own steps is on, and Hey Varnick must be on too; [ADR 0042](0042-a-spoken-request-is-answered-by-the-hosts-voice-session-one-hidden-claude-session-in-home-with-permissions-skipped.md), amended 2026-10-17).
- **Audio goes from the phone to the Mac over the tailnet,** and nowhere else. What the transcriber hears is on-device; what the session reads goes to Anthropic, as ADR 0042 says.
- **One ask at a time per Mac,** the voice session's rule: a request while Hey Varnick's or another phone's is in flight is told the Mac is still on the last one.
- **Amended by [ADR 0044](0044-on-a-mac-hey-varnick-or-a-key-starts-voice-mode-in-which-every-mic-line-is-a-request-until-it-ends.md):** "Voice mode" names the Mac's state as well, started by Hey Varnick or a key and ended by the key, a call, Claude or a minute. The phone's rules here are unchanged: its own Start and End, no minute, no end tool.
- **Amended 2026-10-17 ([ADR 0042](0042-a-spoken-request-is-answered-by-the-hosts-voice-session-one-hidden-claude-session-in-home-with-permissions-skipped.md), see its amendment of that date):** the view shows "Working…" and "Searching the web…" as its state line and no longer as lines of the exchange.
- **Amended 2026-10-17: a request ends only when the user says "over", never on silence (protocol 31).** The rule, its match and its caps are in [ADR 0044](0044-on-a-mac-hey-varnick-or-a-key-starts-voice-mode-in-which-every-mic-line-is-a-request-until-it-ends.md)'s amendment of that date, and this replaces "the page decides where a request ends" above. The page cuts a **segment** (`CONTEXT.md`) at each pause and keeps the microphone live, the Mac collects the segments into the request and asks the voice session at "over" or the cap, and the page shows the request so far as the user's line, still being built. Words said after "over", before the page learns the request was taken, start the next request. The mechanism is in `docs/agents/where-it-breaks.md`, Voice mode on the phone.
  - **Protocol 31, because the old and new pages differ in what they cause:** an older page's whole requests would only be collected by a newer Mac, and a newer page's segments would each be asked of an older Mac. Every Mac, the headless host and each Phone page must run the commit that made protocol 31.
- **Amended 2026-10-31: the view shows the Voice session's earlier exchanges (option 01 of `voice-mode-forgets-the-exchange`).** Before this, "nothing of the exchange is kept" meant closing the view lost it, though the session lives on for 10 minutes. Now the page still keeps none: when the view opens, it asks the Mac for the exchanges its Voice session holds, once, through one new relay request, `voiceExchanges`, and draws them above the live ones under "Earlier in this Voice session". The Mac reads them from the session's own transcript (the conversation the next request continues), each request without the voice hint in front of it and each answer the last text Claude wrote for it, so they are there again after End, a reload, another phone or a restart of Varnick, and include what was asked at the Mac by Hey Varnick. They are shown only for the session the next request continues (`VoiceSession.decide` gives `.send`, or `.busy` while its turn goes on). Once the session has ended, been opened or taken over (ADR 0042), or sits on a question, a plan or an error, past the deadline or idle for 10 minutes, the next request starts a new session and the view opens empty; a session left on a question is reached through Open tabs, where the Phone page lists it marked as the Voice session (the amendment of this date below), or through Needs you. A turn still going shows its request with no answer, since what Claude has written so far is interim; the answer is there at the next open. Only the newest that fit a total are sent, with a line saying how many earlier ones were left out. The view's notes, a request collected and never ended with "over", and refusals are not in the transcript and are not shown again.
  - **No second copy:** nothing new is written on the Mac or the phone. The Mac keeping its own log of what the phone was shown was considered and not chosen: in memory it is lost at a restart while the session lives on, and a file is a second copy of every spoken request. The page keeping the exchange (option 03) was rejected by the user.
  - **Device only.** `voiceExchanges` is a listed device's to send, as `outlineRead` and `phoneAutomations` are: a paired Mac, the host's own page server and a headless host are refused. The Mac's running Varnick answers it on the main actor, so the session read is the one the next request would go to, and reads the transcript off it.
  - **No protocol bump.** The request is new and no request or reply changes shape; only a host's own page server sends it, in the same app as the relay, so the two never differ ([ADR 0040](0040-automations-are-fired-by-the-host-that-keeps-them-and-a-run-is-a-pane-kept-out-of-sight-until-opened.md)'s amendment of 2026-10-29 took this course at the user's word). A running Varnick older than its relay answers "Unknown request", and the view says it couldn't read the earlier exchanges. Protocol stays 34.
  - **What it shows the phone:** what was asked at the desk now shows on any listed device that opens Voice mode on that Mac's page. A device could already open the session's terminal through Needs you, so this is no new kind of reach; `docs/privacy-and-security.md` says so.
- **Amended 2026-10-31: a Mac's Phone page lists the Voice session, marked (option 02 of `voice-mode-forgets-the-exchange`).** This replaces what the ADR said above, that the hidden tab is in no tab list and is reached by the needs-you event alone. Open tabs lists it from when its first request is taken until it ends, so the user can see it is still going after Voice mode ends and open it. Its row says **Voice session** and "Runs with permissions skipped", and it is never a run: it has no run mark, so it is not under Runs and the other Macs' rule for runs does not apply to it.
  - **A listed device is told, nothing else is.** The Mac's `tabs` reply carries a new optional `voice` mark on that one tab, and the relay leaves the marked tab out for a paired Mac and for the host's own page server, which reads the listing for its push watch and port proxy. Other Macs do not list it and no push is sent for its turns, as before. A headless host has no Voice session and lists none.
  - **Watching takes nothing.** Opening it shows the live screen as any session's, and Voice mode and Hey Varnick go on typing into it. Before Take over, the tab view says that Take over makes the session the user's, that Varnick then stops using it, and that it runs with permissions skipped until it is restarted.
  - **Take over makes it the user's, by ADR 0042's rule, unchanged.** Varnick no longer types into it or ends it, the next spoken request starts a new Voice session, the phone lists it as an ordinary tab, and option 01's earlier exchanges are in this session's terminal and not in Voice mode. Done on its row closes it on the Mac, which ends it.
  - **Listed from its first turn.** A session that never took its request is never listed, and one that stopped on a question is.
  - **No protocol bump.** The `tabs` reply gains an optional mark on one tab, and only a listed device's reply has it; that listing is asked only by the host's own page server, in the same app as the relay, and a paired Mac's reply is unchanged, so no two builds read it differently ([ADR 0040](0040-automations-are-fired-by-the-host-that-keeps-them-and-a-run-is-a-pane-kept-out-of-sight-until-opened.md)'s amendment of 2026-10-29 took this course at the user's word). Until the Mac's Varnick restarts on the build, a page server still running from before passes the tab through without its mark, so the row shows unmarked. Protocol stays 34.
  - **What it shows the phone:** a listed device could already reach the session by speaking to it and by Needs you's Open when a question was pending; the list adds a way in that needs no question pending. The consent is the one the session already needs, Hey Varnick on that Mac; `docs/privacy-and-security.md` says so.
